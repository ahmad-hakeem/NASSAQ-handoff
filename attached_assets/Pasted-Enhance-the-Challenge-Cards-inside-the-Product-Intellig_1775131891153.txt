==================================================

  → prominently displayed
  → easy to identify at a glance

==================================================

- Place the reporter name in a prominent position inside the card:

  → top section of the card (near title)

==================================================

  → quickly know who created the challenge
  → understand ownership and context

  → show additional info (email / role)

- enhance clarity
