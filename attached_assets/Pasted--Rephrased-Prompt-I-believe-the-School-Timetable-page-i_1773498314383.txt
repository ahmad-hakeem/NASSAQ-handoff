All important timetable data must be retrieved **exclusively from the database**, specifically from the data configured in:

**School Settings**

* Academic year
* Current term
* School days
* Number of daily periods
* Period duration
* Break times
* Prayer times
* Classes
* Teachers
* Subjects
* Teacher assignments

No timetable data should be hardcoded or locally defined in the page.
